Who We Are

A beacon of academic excellence in the educational landscape of Firozabad

Established in 2017, Delhi Public School Firozabad has rapidly grown into a leading centre of academic excellence and holistic development. Spread over 11+ acres in Daulatpur with sprawling green lawns, spacious classrooms and open environs, the campus offers an ambience conducive to intellectual pursuits. Child-centric education stresses integrated learning experiences with equal weight to academics, games and sports, art, heritage, environment and culture.

  • Rigorous CBSE curriculum from Nursery to Class X, delivered in English medium with a blend of academics and co-curriculars.
  • Board, District & Mandal Toppers for four consecutive years, with a 100% pass rate in Class X board examinations (2020–21, 2021–22).
  • Modern infrastructure — AR/VR lab, smart classrooms, physics, chemistry, biology, maths and computer-science labs, library, music and dance rooms.
  • Holistic sports programme — cricket, swimming, taekwondo and physical-education coaches on faculty.
  • DPS Society pedigree — part of the Delhi Public School Society family with a centralized school-management framework.
